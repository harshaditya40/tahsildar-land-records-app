The End-to-End Mutation Journey

Under the AP Rights in Land and Pattadar Pass Books Act, every transaction follows a legally binding, audit-proof progression.

01
Application

Form 6-A & SRO Trigger

Citizen files Form 6-A on the portal or SRO deed registration automatically dispatches an electronic mutation notice into the Mandal revenue queue.

02
Verification

Field Demarcation

Mandal Cadastral Surveyor conducts on-ground inspection with RTK DGPS & Drone telemetry (±1.2 cm accuracy), verifying zero boundary overlap with neighbors.

03
Quasi-Judicial

15-Day Public Notice

Statutory notice auto-published under Sec 5(3). Tahasildar scrutinizes sale instruments, legal heir genealogies, and verifies absence of civil court injunctions.

04
Record Mutation

e-Sign & Passbook Update

Tahasildar executes quasi-judicial order with cryptographic PKI e-Sign (`DS-AP-REV-2026-..`). Webland 2.0 and cadastral layer instantly updated with new Form 1-B.

National Alignment

Grounded in Law, Powered by Geospatial Intelligence

TAHSILDAR is built strictly around the Andhra Pradesh Rights in Land and Pattadar Pass Books Act, 1971 read with the Government of India's Digital India Land Record Modernisation Programme (DILRMP).

By combining 14-digit ULPIN (Bhu-Aadhaar) indexing with sub-centimeter RTK GNSS field surveys and Turf.js geodesic polygon bisection (Pothissing), the system eliminates fraudulent double registrations and boundary disputes.

  • Statutory 15-Day Public Notice Protocol (Sec. 5(3))
  • Interoperable with SRO Sub-Registrar Deed ledgers
  • Drone DGPS RTK survey integration with GCP precision
  • Instant Form 1-B e-Pattadar Passbook generation with QR verification
